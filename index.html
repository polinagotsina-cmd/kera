#1  What if I need to take a call?

Use a call booth or a quiet zone. No one will hear you, and you won’t hear them.

#2 Will my Wi-Fi drop mid-call?

No. We have fast fiber Wi-Fi with a backup line, so if one goes down, the other one takes over.

#3 Where do I charge my laptop?

At your desk. Every desk has its own socket, no hunting for an outlet.

#4 Can I take a break?

Of course. There’s a lounge, a kitchen with a fridge and a microwave, and free coffee, tea and water.

#5 Is it comfortable to sit all day?

That’s the whole idea: proper desks and chairs made for a full working day, not café stools.

#6 Can I come early or stay late?

We’re open every day from 8 am to 10 pm, so early birds and night owls are both welcome.

#7 Do I need to book?

No booking needed, just walk in. Your first day is free.

Built by remote workers,
for remote workers

We moved to Tbilisi in 2022 to work
remotely, and quickly ran out
of places
to do it. Cafés had slow
Wi-Fi and one
socket for the whole
room. Home was too
quiet, then
too loud. Other coworkings felt
like
offices nobody wanted to be in.

So we opened KERA: a calm,
reliable space
with everything
you need to get through
a full day
of work. Fast internet, a socket
at every desk, quiet corners
for calls
and a kitchen for breaks.
